Data and caveats

  • Payments come from the "ad rev details sep" sheet: 64 outlet rows, 25 chain IDs, 1,064,613 in total, September only.
  • GMV comes from the October workbook, not a fresh Looker export. It covers May to September, and the October column looks like a run-rate projection.
  • Orders exist only for October 1 to 3, so churn is judged on GMV, not monthly orders.
  • The three acceptance rates are assumptions, not measured behaviour. Adjust them above.
  • Scenario C goes beyond the strict 1%, 1.5%, 2% tiers: 14 vendors already paying above 2% keep their actual amount.
  • A manually typed ask is counted as a custom amount. Risk-adjusted NCR treats it like any other ask: 95% of what a vendor already pays, 50% of an increase, 25% for vendors who never paid.
  • Snack and Go has October GMV but no orders on October 1 to 3, so its data may not match.
Figures are in EGP. Typed asks reset when you reload the page; Feedback notes are kept in this browser.
